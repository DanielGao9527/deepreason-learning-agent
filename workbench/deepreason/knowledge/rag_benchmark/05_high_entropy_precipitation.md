# RAG Benchmark: High Entropy Alloy Precipitation
Document id: rag_benchmark_high_entropy_precipitation.

High entropy alloy strength can be affected by precipitation hardening, solid solution strengthening, grain size, dislocation density, phase stability, stacking fault energy, and processing history. Nanoscale precipitates can increase strength when they impede dislocation motion, but excessive brittle phases may reduce ductility.

中文说明：高熵合金强度与析出强化、固溶强化、晶粒尺寸、位错密度、相稳定性、层错能和加工历史有关。纳米析出相可能提高强度，但过多脆性相会损伤塑性。
