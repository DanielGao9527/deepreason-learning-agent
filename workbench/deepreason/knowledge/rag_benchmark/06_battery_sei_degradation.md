# RAG Benchmark: Battery SEI Degradation
Document id: rag_benchmark_battery_sei_degradation.

Lithium-ion battery capacity fade can be driven by solid electrolyte interphase growth, lithium inventory loss, cathode transition-metal dissolution, electrolyte oxidation, particle cracking, and impedance rise. SEI thickening consumes cyclable lithium and increases resistance.

中文说明：锂离子电池容量衰减可能来自 SEI 膜持续生长、活性锂损失、正极过渡金属溶解、电解液氧化、颗粒开裂和阻抗升高。
